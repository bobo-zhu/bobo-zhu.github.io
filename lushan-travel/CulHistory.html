Huiyuan (334-416), surnamed Jia, a high monk in the Eastern Jin Dynasty, was the ancestor of the Pure Land Sect. Emperor Wu of Jin Dynasty went to Lushan in the eighth year of Taiyuan (383) to cultivate oneself, promote Taoism and preach Buddhism for more than 30 years. During the period, Donglin Temple was founded, the white lotus society was established, and books were written to make Donglin Temple the center of southern Buddhism.
