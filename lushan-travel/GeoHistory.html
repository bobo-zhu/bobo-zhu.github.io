Glacier

The glaciers produced during the Quaternary Pleistocene (300 to 10,000 years) are called Quaternary glaciers. Glaciers in the Quaternary Holocene (10,000 to 100,000 years) are called modern glaciers. 3 million years ago, when the Quaternary Great Ice Age came, Lushan was already a lonely mountain standing upright. At that time, the height of the snow line was about 1000-1200 meters, and the amount of snowfall was large, forming the piedmont glacier. Mount Lu is the most abundantly preserved area of Quaternary glacial relics in eastern China. During the Quaternary Great Ice Age, Lushan had four glacial periods and one periglacial period, namely the Da Pailing Glacial Period (3 to 2.5 million years), Poyang Glacial Period (1.8 to 1.5 million years), and Dagu Glacial Period (110 ~900,000 years), Lushan glacial period (400,000 to 200,000 years), Lulin marginal glacial period (120,000 to 10,000 years). Due to the well-developed and typical Quaternary glacier relics of Mount Lushan, and the research level is relatively high, it has become the standard area for the classification and comparison of Quaternary glaciers in China. Mount Lu is the foundation of China's Quaternary Glacial Geology.
The main types of Quaternary glacial relics in Mount Lushan are composed of glacier erosion relics and glacial mud and gravel moraines. Glacier erosion relics refer to the traces left by the excavation and erosion of the mountain during the formation and movement of the glacier, which are mainly composed of ice buckets, horns, suspended valleys, U-shaped valleys, blade ridges, ice cellars, ice ridges, and ice steps. , Ice basin and glacier scratches. The gravel size in the glacial mud moraine is unsorted and mixed with mud and sand, and there are also glacial boulders (huge rocks brought from outside by glaciers). Ice Table

"Ice table" is a long table-shaped independent rock composed of Sinian feldspar quartz sandstone. The stone body is slippery, and the texture of the upper and lower boulders is very inconsistent. The two stones stand aloof and become a spectacle, which is called "flying stone". Mr. Li Siguang classified it as a glacial boulder, and vividly called it "Ice Table".

U-shaped valley

Glacial U-shaped valleys are often referred to as glacial troughs, valleys or glacial valleys. Among the dozens of glacial valleys preserved in Mount Lu, the clearest and most typical ones are Wangjiapo Valley, Daxiaochang Valley, Qilichong Valley, and Donggu.

Corner peak

Jiaofeng is a pyramid-shaped peak surrounded by three or more ice buckets or ice cellars, icy depressions, and U-shaped valleys with steep rock walls.
